title: human strange nature
date: 2026-08-14
---
I've always wanted to be heard, wanted to be seen—but not in the way we normally mean it.

It's strange. I don't want to talk to so many people. I don't even want to share everything with anyone. I don't want to go out. I avoid talking to people as much as I can.

And yet, somehow, I still want to be seen.
