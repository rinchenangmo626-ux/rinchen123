# My Written Life

This is your personal public-writing website starter.

## Files

- `index.html` — the page and your writing/content
- `style.css` — appearance and layout
- `script.js` — small interactive features

## To preview it

Open `index.html` in a browser.

## To add your writing

The six cards in `index.html` are starter pieces. Replace their titles, descriptions, and `data-text` values with your own writing.

Later, we can turn each piece into its own page and publish the whole site publicly on the internet.
